VENTURE FUND MODE

Run a $10M fund for ten years. Find the deal that returns it.

  • Each half-year a few fictional startups pitch. Back the ones you believe in with a cheque now and a reserve for later rounds.
  • When portfolio companies raise again, take your pro-rata or get diluted.
  • Outcomes follow a power law: most companies fail, a few return 50x or more.
  • Fees (2% a year) and carry (20%) come out before your LPs see a dollar.
